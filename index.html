Getting started: your first check-in becomes your baseline. From day two, this page will show how your Happiness and Development scores move together — and what that tells you.
A question worth asking daily
"How do I actually feel today —
and is my life still moving where I want it to?"
Two different questions. Most days we only answer one, or neither. INGRID asks both — every day, in three minutes — so the pattern between them becomes visible instead of invisible.
2
indices, not one score
The Happiness Index is about today: sleep, body, mood, presence. The Development Index is about direction: clarity, self-acceptance, structure, growth, connection. Feeling good and moving forward are not the same thing — INGRID tracks both.
3
minutes a day
Nine questions. Two numbers. That's it. Less time than it takes to put the phone down for the night.
30
days to see the pattern
A month in, you'll start to see which days you felt good but weren't really moving — and which days felt hard but were exactly the growth you needed.
"Feeling good and going somewhere are two different questions. Answer both, honestly, and the direction takes care of itself."
